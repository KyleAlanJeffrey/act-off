Before you audition

Questions from the cast

What is Dub-Off?

A free browser game where you re-voice famous movie scenes. You hear each original line, record your own take over the muted clip, and watch the whole scene back with your voice swapped in — the film's music stays underneath.

What do I need to play?

A desktop browser and a microphone. No account, no install, nothing uploaded — recording and playback all happen on your machine.

Can I play with friends?

Solo Show — one player voices every character — is out now. Party mode with a lobby, one character per player, and a Favorite Voice vote is in the works.

Can I keep my dub?

Yes — after the screening, download your cut as a video file or share it straight from the browser.
